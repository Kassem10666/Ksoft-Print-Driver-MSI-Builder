Printer Settings & Configuration
Package preset printer hardware options, paper trays, duplex, and color preferences.
💡 How to Export Printer Settings
📄 1. Export .DAT Binary Settings (PrintUIEntry)
Exports full driver properties, hardware trays, paper sizes, and device defaults from an installed printer (Run in CMD / PowerShell as Admin):
rundll32 printui.dll,PrintUIEntry /Ss /n "PrinterName" /a "C:\Settings.dat"
📑 2. Export .XML PrintTicket (PowerShell)
Exports duplex (2-sided), color / black & white, orientation, and resolution preferences natively:
Get-PrintConfiguration -PrinterName "PrinterName" | Export-Clixml -Path "C:\Settings.xml"
💡 Note: Replace "PrinterName" with the exact name of your configured reference printer. You can upload .dat, .xml, or bundle both in a .zip.
